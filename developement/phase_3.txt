EDUSPHERE CLASSROOM - PHASE 3 COMPLETION SUMMARY

Phase 3 has successfully integrated advanced AI capabilities and finalized critical Phase 2 features. 

Below is a comprehensive list of everything implemented:

1. AI-POWERED FEATURES
----------------------
* Sentiment Analysis: Automatic sentiment classification for all forum posts and comments to help teachers identify student sentiment and potential issues.
* Early Warning System: An AI-driven risk assessment engine that identifies at-risk students based on engagement patterns (attendance, submissions, forum activity).
* At-Risk Students Widget: A teacher-facing dashboard component to proactively monitor students requiring support.
* Smart Content Assistance: 
    - AI-generated summaries for class materials and resources.
    - AI-suggested tags for better organization and discoverability of content.
* Personalized Recommendations: A "Students Also Viewed" system that helps students discover relevant learning materials based on topic and peer behavior.
* Lesson Plan Generator: A powerful AI tool for teachers to generate structured lesson plans from topics, grade levels, and objectives within seconds.

2. PHASE 2 REMAINDER & REFINEMENTS

    - Implementation of an asynchronous notification queue using BullMQ and Redis.
    - Reliable email dispatch groundwork for production environments.
    - Multi-channel support (In-App, Email, and placeholder for Push).
* Notification Settings Fix:
    - Resolved a critical runtime crash in the settings page.

* Peer Review Enhancements:
    - Improved randomization logic for peer review assignments.

3. INFRASTRUCTURE & DATABASE
----------------------------
* Schema Expansion: Added new tables for `studentRisk`, `resourceRecommendations`, and `parentInvitations`.
